THE TOUR

See what Editarr does

Your Jellyfin library

Browse the libraries you can see in Jellyfin and pick the exact version to work on.

Scan with local AI

Audio and visual models run on your own host. Nothing leaves your server.

One organized queue

Name your scans and follow them from anywhere in the app.

Review and refine

Preview every finding, keep what matters to you, and add your own filters.

Filtered subtitles

Redact filtered words from subtitles to match what you mute.

Publish for every viewer

Write sidecar files beside the video. The Jellyfin EDL plugin serves each viewer their own.

Community filters

Start from a master filter set shared by others. You approve the alignment before it’s used. Contribute your filters back.

Built for households

Everyone signs in with their own Jellyfin account. Reviews and exports stay personal.
